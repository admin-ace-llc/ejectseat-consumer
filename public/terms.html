What EjectSeat is

EjectSeat is an analytical product that reads public SEC filings, earnings call transcripts, and Tier A/B media coverage and returns a predictive risk score. The score is a signal, not a prediction. It is not investment advice, not legal advice, and not a forecast of what will happen to any specific employee.

What EjectSeat isn't

We are not a financial advisor. We do not recommend any action regarding your employment, compensation, or financial planning. We do not have access to internal company information; everything we use is public.

Accuracy

We make best-effort attempts to read and analyse the available public information correctly. Markets and company circumstances change; historical signals do not guarantee future outcomes; and our analysis may be wrong. Use EjectSeat as one input among many, alongside your own judgement and any direct information you have.
