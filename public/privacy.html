Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 April 2026

EjectSeat is operated by Admin-Ace LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This page explains, in plain English, what personal data we collect when you use the service at ejectseat.io, why we collect it, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to sign in (magic link) or subscribe to score-change alerts.
  • Search history — the company names you search for, the resulting score, and the timestamp. This powers your watchlist, the "recents" chips, and our aggregate analytics.
  • Anonymous session identifier — a random ID stored in a first-party cookie (es_session) so we can give consistent responses across page loads even if you don't sign in.
  • Basic request metadata — page views, referrer (where you came from), and a truncated user-agent string. We do not store IP addresses for analytics.

We do not collect: names, phone numbers, payment information, location data, employer details, or any other personal information. The service is free and there is no payment processing.

Why we collect it

  • Magic-link sign-in — to authenticate you without passwords.
  • Caching scores — so we don't refetch SEC filings every time you re-search a company within 6–24 hours.
  • Aggregate analytics — to understand which companies are popular and to power the "LandingZone" hiring view based on collective search patterns.
  • Score-change alerts (opt-in) — to email you if a company on your watchlist has a score change above your chosen threshold.

Who we share it with

We use the following third-party processors to operate the service. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication system. Your email and search history are stored on Supabase infrastructure (US region).
  • Vercel — hosts the website and runs the API. They process incoming requests but do not retain your data.
  • Anthropic — we send public-filing text and news headlines to Anthropic's Claude API for language analysis. Your personal data (email, session ID) is never sent to Anthropic.
  • Adzuna — provides the live job listings shown in the "LandingZone" section. We send a company name and role/location filters; we do not send any personal data.
  • SEC EDGAR & Google News RSS — public data sources we read. We send only the company name, no personal data.

Cookies

We use one first-party cookie (es_session) to identify your browser session. It expires after 365 days. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics pixels (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no Hotjar, etc.).

Data retention

  • Search history — kept indefinitely so we can show you trends over time. Delete on request.
  • Score cache — refreshed automatically every 6 hours (signed-in) or 24 hours (anonymous).
  • Anonymous session data — cleared when you delete your browser cookies.
  • Email-based account — kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccuracies
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Stop sending you any emails

To exercise any of these rights, email enquiries.talkace@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

EjectSeat is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be notified via email to signed-in users.
