How EjectSeat works

Public sources only

We read SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 20-F, 6-K), earnings call transcripts, and Tier A/B business media (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, CNBC). No insider data.

Four risk states

CLEAR — no material signals. WATCH — forward indicators present. LIKELY — multiple corroborating signals. ACTIVE — confirmed event within 90 days or programme in motion.

Strict evidence binding

Every claim in the analysis must cite a specific filing accession number or article URL from the evidence bundle. Unattributed claims are stripped automatically.

A signal, not a prediction

EjectSeat is a predictive signal — not a guarantee of what will happen. Use it as one input alongside your own judgement and any direct information you have.
